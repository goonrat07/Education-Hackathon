# Sample materials

Two files for trying Study Coach end to end. Add a Chemistry test, then upload both:

| File | Tag it as | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `unit-4-moles-study-guide.pdf` | Teacher material | A three-page typed study guide on the mole and stoichiometry, with a table, a diagram and worked examples. |
| `my-notes-limiting-reactant.jpg` | My notes | One page of handwritten-style notes on limiting reactants and percent yield, with a drawn diagram. |

These were made for the first build of the app because the repository had no
`samples/` folder at the time. The notes page is set in a handwriting typeface
and photographed-looking, not a scan of real handwriting. Replace or add to
them with your own handouts and photos of your notes whenever you like.
